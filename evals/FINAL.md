## Limits

- Test accuracy is above validation accuracy, most likely because the data differs. The dataset's authors reviewed the test images by hand and left noise, including some wrong labels, in the training images on purpose. Validation images come from the training images, and real uploads may be messier than the test set too.
- Sampling alone could move the test top-1 by about 0.34 points at 95% confidence, and a validation top-1 by about 0.73 points. Gaps between runs smaller than that may be noise.
- Every run used one seed.
- Latency comes from one core of a desktop CPU, which may be faster than a vCPU in the cloud.
